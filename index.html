BeeHeard Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

BeeHeard is a voice training game that helps users practice and improve their ability to hold vocal tones. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when using the BeeHeard application.

1. Data Processing

BeeHeard is designed to work locally on your device. The application does not require an account, registration, or login.

BeeHeard does not upload, store, or transmit your voice recordings or personal information to external servers.

2. Microphone Usage

BeeHeard requires microphone access during voice training sessions. The microphone is used only to analyze your voice in real time, including characteristics such as pitch and loudness, to provide feedback and control the gameplay.

Audio recordings are not created, stored, or shared. Voice data is processed locally on your device and discarded after processing.

3. Locally Stored Data

BeeHeard stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Training statistics
  • Game progress
  • Application settings
  • Premium purchase status

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to BeeHeard or any third party. You can delete locally stored data at any time by clearing the application data or uninstalling the application.

4. In-App Purchases

BeeHeard uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. BeeHeard does not receive or store payment information such as credit card details.

5. Third-Party Services

BeeHeard does not use analytics services, advertising services, tracking technologies, or external data collection services.

6. Data Sharing

BeeHeard does not sell, share, or transfer personal information to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

BeeHeard does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, the application does not create user profiles or online accounts.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
